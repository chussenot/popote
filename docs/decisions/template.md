# Decision record template

Copy this file, keep the headings, replace the guidance. Set the frontmatter
`title` and the H1 to the decision stated as a sentence ("mise is the single
toolchain entry point"), and `type: decision`.

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The problem, the forces at play and the constraints, in a few sentences. A
reader who was not there should understand why a decision was needed.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly. One paragraph.

## Alternatives

Each option rejected, and the specific reason. "Not considered" is not an
alternative.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what it costs, and what observation
would make you revisit the decision.
